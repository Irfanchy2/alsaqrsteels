# Editing the website

Sign in at **/admin/login** with your staff account. Changes appear on the public site within about a minute (pages are refreshed automatically after each save).

## Enquiries

**Enquiries** lists every quote request, newest first, with its reference (`ASQ-2026-01001`), service, status and whether the email notification went out.

Open one to see the full scope, contact details and attachments. Attachment links are private and expire after 10 minutes — open them from the CMS each time. Set the status (New → Reviewing → Quoted → Won / Lost / Closed, or Spam) and keep internal notes; customers never see these. If the email notification shows **failed**, press **Retry email**. Delete an enquiry only when the customer asks for their data to be removed.

## Projects

Each project has a title, code (PRJ-01…), status (completed / ongoing / tender), sector, location, emirate, year, services involved, scope and description.

Rules the site is built around:

- **Client name** is stored privately. Tick **Show client publicly** only when the client has agreed in writing.
- Add **photos only of the actual job**. Without photos the page shows "Photographs being prepared" — that is intended, not an error. Never use stock or landmark images.
- Every photo needs a short description (alt text) of what it shows, e.g. "Pipe rack section being lifted into place at Taweelah". Photos should be at least 1600 px on the long side; JPG or PNG under 10 MB.
- Untick **Published** to hide a project without deleting it.

## Services

Edit the title, summary, introduction, scope list (one item per line), applications and SEO title/description. The photo is optional; services without a genuine photo use the line drawing.

## FAQs

Questions can be attached to the home page, the quote page or a specific service. Keep answers factual; do not state prices or lead times.

## Quality & certifications

A certificate appears on the public Quality page only when **all** of these are true: it is in the company's name, a scan is uploaded, it is marked **Verified**, and its expiry date has not passed. Expired certificates hide themselves automatically.

## Documents

Upload the company profile (PDF) here. The "Download company profile" button appears on the site only once a published document exists.

## Company details

Phone numbers, WhatsApp, email, address, hours, social links and hero text. The WhatsApp button uses this number, and the phone links use the international format automatically.

## Writing tips

Write the way you would explain the job to a site engineer: what was made, from what, how big, where it went. Avoid claims you cannot show on paper ("best", "leading", "100%"). If a fact is uncertain, leave it out — the pages are designed to read well without it.
